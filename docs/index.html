The flow

A comment's journey

From the moment a comment lands on your page to a final decision — in seconds. No content is ever deleted; problematic posts are hidden with an appeal link.

STAGE 1 · FAST

⚡ Claude Haiku

Lightning-fast first pass. If confidence is ≥ 80%, the decision is final and returned immediately.

allow hide
→
STAGE 2 · DEEP

🧠 Claude Sonnet

Re-evaluates uncertain cases with full context. If still below threshold, escalates to human.

allow hide reportable
→
STAGE 3 · HUMAN

🧑‍⚖️ Human review

Moderator sees the AI's reasoning and decides. Every decision is logged with who made it.

final decision
BEFORE AI — GATE CHECK

Webhook HMAC-SHA256 verification, identity resolution, banned-user fast-path, duplicate SHA-256 fingerprint detection.

RECIDIVISM TRACKING

Violations accumulate. Exceeding the threshold triggers progressive bans (configurable): 1-hour → 1-day → 7-day

APPEALS WORKFLOW

Signed HMAC URL (30-day expiry) lets users contest any hidden comment. Moderators accept (restores) or reject.

Features

Everything you need
out of the box

Production-ready features included — not bolted on later.

Context-aware AI

Account age, follower count, and internal violation history are sent as context to every AI call. Smarter decisions, fewer false positives.

Advanced threat detection

Scam patterns (pig butchering, fake giveaways, wallet theft), grooming signals, coordinated spam — trained policy, not just keyword lists.

Versioned policy management

Edit your moderation rules from the UI. Every change is versioned, audit-logged, and reversible. Activate any version instantly.

Human review queue

Prioritized dashboard showing AI reasoning per comment. Moderators see confidence scores, categories, and full context before deciding.

Progressive ban system

Repeated violations escalate automatically. The ban is internal — no notifications to Facebook, no drama. Full history preserved.

Multi-page & OAuth2 login

Connect multiple Facebook pages in one click. First user auto-becomes admin. Google, Meta, and Microsoft OAuth2 supported.

Full audit trail

Every AI and human decision is logged with model name, confidence score, latency, categories, and the name of the human moderator.

Configurable data retention

Nightly cron anonymizes PII after N days. Statistical fields preserved for analytics. Reset operational data without losing configuration.

Appeals & transparency

Hidden comments get a signed appeal link. Users can contest; moderators accept or reject. Every AI decision comes with its reasoning — nothing is a black box.

Special handling

Three dedicated lanes

Some content types need more than a binary allow/hide decision.

🚨 Reportable content

Potentially illegal content is hidden immediately and routed to a dedicated "Reports" queue for legal review. Never deleted — preserved as evidence.

📰 Editorial criticism

Criticism of journalists or the publication always goes to human review — protecting legitimate editorial debate from over-moderation.

🔎 Fact-check

Dubious but not malicious claims: the AI drafts a reply with sources, a human reviews and decides whether to post or hide.

Why I built this
This tool is not for silencing dissent. It is not for filtering out criticism, inconvenient opinions, or voices that make you uncomfortable. If that's what you're looking for, this is the wrong tool — and honestly, the wrong approach. Moderation exists to protect conversation, not to control it.

I don't use social media. I haven't for years.
But I listen. And what I kept hearing — in evening conversations with my wife, with friends and families, with people genuinely trying to make sense of a difficult world — was frustration. Comment sections on news pages had become places of noise, hostility, and exhaustion. Journalists and editors were spending time they didn't have reading through hundreds of comments just to find the ones that actually needed attention.

My children are now reaching the age when social media becomes part of the world. I hope that when they step into it, the conversation they find will be worth joining.

I'm not a journalist. I'm not an activist. I'm someone who believes that money shapes too much of this world — and that the world should be shaped by ideas instead. By the freedom to discuss them seriously, without prejudice, without someone's business interests deciding what gets heard and what doesn't.

So I built something free. Open source. Self-hosted. Not because it was easier — it wasn't — but because the tool itself needed to reflect the values behind it.

Social Moderation Hub is a two-stage AI pipeline that helps anyone who manages a public conversation at scale — newsrooms protecting editorial integrity, social media managers drowning in daily volume, marketing teams safeguarding brand reputation, community managers trying to keep spaces worth being in. Fast, lightweight, and designed to escalate to human review only when it truly matters — because the hard decisions should always stay human.

No vendor lock-in. No data sent to third-party servers. No subscription that quietly shapes what the tool does or doesn't flag.

Solo una cosa utile, costruita con cura, regalata liberamente.

Just a useful thing, built carefully, given away freely.
